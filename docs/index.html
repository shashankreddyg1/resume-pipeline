Resume Pipeline

Scraper finds the job → this page tailors your resume to it → ready to apply. Everything runs in your browser: your profile and your API key never leave this page. Get a free Gemini key at Google AI Studio.

1 · Your profile

Your real facts as JSON (name, contact, experience bullets, projects, education). Stays in your browser.

2 · The job

…or paste the description manually below. Other sites block auto-fetch — the paste box always works.

3 · AI rewrite rules

The exact contract the AI gets. Edit it if you like — the defaults never invent facts. Saved in your browser.

4 · Resume template

Your template, your rules — all editable, all saved in your browser. If your profile JSON has a "skills" list, it overrides the skill groups below.
